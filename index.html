Guide

Definitions + visual demos together. Open an assessment to see exactly what counts as PASS and how the movement should look.
Deep Squat
  • Comfortable full-depth squat.
  • Both feet stay planted.
  • Balance without support.
  • Stand up under control.
PASS: 1 clean successful rep within 3 attempts.
Hamstring Mobility
  • Knees straight but not aggressively locked.
  • Fold forward under control.
  • Palms near the floor.
  • No bouncing or external assistance.
  • Hold ~2 seconds.
PASS: 1 successful controlled hold within 3 attempts.
Straddle Flexibility
  • Legs at least 90° apart.
  • Both knees remain straight.
  • Kneecaps and toes stay generally facing upward.
  • Lean forward until both forearms are flat on the floor between your legs.
  • Hold for 10 seconds.
  • No bouncing, pulling yourself down, or external assistance.
PASS: 1 successful 10-second hold within 3 attempts.
Hip & Ankle Mobility
  • 90/90 hip position on both sides, hold 10 seconds.
  • Knee-to-wall on both ankles from about 10 cm.
  • Heel stays flat and foot stays controlled.
PASS: both hips + both ankles pass.
High-Kick Mobility
  • Front kick: striking foot to about nose height.
  • Side kick: heel to about shoulder height.
  • Both legs; controlled extension and return.
  • No jumping or external assistance.
PASS: front + side kick on both legs, up to 3 attempts each.
Jumps & Landings — Control Challenge
Squat Jump10 reps
Forward Broad Jump5 reps • hold landing ~2 sec
Lateral Jumps5 each direction
Step / Drop Landing5 reps from ~20–30 cm
  • 3 rounds.
  • Rest 60–90 sec between rounds.
PASS: all 3 rounds; controlled reasonably quiet landings; good knee tracking; no falls; no extra recovery steps.
Plyometrics — Performance Challenge
Pogo Jumps10 continuous reps
Squat Jumps8 continuous reps
Broad Jumps5 powerful reps
Skater Bounds10 total • 5 each side
Low Box Jumps5 reps • 20–30 cm • step down
  • 5 rounds.
  • 60 sec rest between rounds.
PASS: all 5 rounds; explosiveness and controlled landings maintained; progressive technique breakdown = fail.
Martial Arts — Beginner
  • Recognised martial art with structured training.
  • Basic stance/guard, footwork, attacks and defence.
  • Controlled combinations and safe training etiquette.
PASS: competent demonstration of your school’s beginner curriculum.
Martial Arts — Intermediate
  • Reliable fundamentals and more advanced combinations.
  • Improved movement, defence, balance, speed and control.
  • Ability to execute under increasing pressure.
PASS: competency consistent with your school’s intermediate curriculum. School/instructor standard takes priority over belt colour.
Sparring — Competent
  • Maintains guard and movement under pressure.
  • Attacks and defends with appropriate technique.
  • Controls distance, power and intensity.
  • Maintains composure and partner safety.
  • Completes multiple rounds without total technique collapse.
PASS: instructor/coach considers you competent to spar at your current training level.
