Glossary

Your rulebook for the movement and fighting requirements used in Levels.
Deep Squat
What it means
  • Comfortable full-depth squat.
  • Shows usable hip, knee and ankle range.
Pass
  • Reach a deep squat under control.
  • Keep both feet planted.
  • Maintain balance without support.
Hamstring Mobility
What it means
  • Enough posterior-chain range to hinge toward the floor comfortably.
  • No bouncing or forcing the position.
Level target
  • Palms near the floor with controlled form.
Flexibility
Level target
  • Palms flat on the floor.
  • Use controlled form.
  • No bouncing or forcing the stretch.
Hip & Ankle Mobility
90/90 — LEFT SIDE
90/90 — RIGHT SIDE

What it means

  • Hip mobility: use the 90/90 position on both sides to check controlled internal and external hip rotation.
  • Ankle mobility: use a knee-to-wall test to check ankle dorsiflexion.

How to test the ankle

  • Face a wall with the test foot flat and pointing forward.
  • Drive the knee forward until it touches the wall while keeping the heel completely down.
  • Start with the toes about 10 cm from the wall. If the knee cannot touch without the heel lifting, move the foot slightly closer and repeat.
  • The test is about how far the knee can travel over the toes without the heel lifting or the foot collapsing inward.
  • Repeat on both sides and compare control and range.

Level 15 pass

  • Complete the 90/90 hip position comfortably on both sides.
  • Aim for approximately 10 cm toe-to-wall on both sides: knee touches the wall, heel stays flat, foot remains controlled and there is no major compensation.
High-Kick Mobility
What it means
  • Active range of motion for useful martial-arts kicking positions.
  • This is a mobility test, not a fighting-proficiency test.
  • Test both legs.
Kick targets
  • Front kick: striking foot reaches your own nose height.
  • Side kick: heel reaches your own shoulder height.
Pass
  • Reach the required height under control.
  • No jumping.
  • No external assistance.
  • Judge height using the striking part of the foot.
Jumps & Landings
Squat Jump10 reps
Forward Broad Jump5 reps • hold each landing ~2 sec

Step / Drop Landing5 reps from ~20–30 cm

Level 17 — Control
  • 10 squat jumps.
  • 5 forward broad jumps.
  • Hold each broad-jump landing for ~2 seconds.
  • 5 lateral jumps each direction.
  • 5 step/drop landings from ~20–30 cm.
Pass
  • Controlled landings.
  • Reasonably quiet landings.
  • Knees track well.
  • No falls.
  • No extra recovery steps.
Plyometrics
Pogo Jumps10 continuous reps
Continuous Squat Jumps8 continuous reps
Powerful Broad Jumps5 reps
Skater Bounds10 reps • 5 each side
Low Box Jumps5 reps from ~20–30 cm • step down
Level 18 — Explosiveness & Reactivity
  • 10 continuous pogo jumps.
  • 8 continuous squat jumps.
  • 5 powerful broad jumps.
  • 10 skater bounds — 5 each side.
  • 5 low box jumps from ~20–30 cm.
  • Step down between box-jump reps rather than jumping down.
Pass
  • Keep technique consistent.
  • Maintain landing control.
  • No major loss of balance.
  • Form should not progressively break down.
Martial Arts — Beginner
Requirement
  • Train in a recognised martial art.
  • Demonstrate beginner-level technique and competency.
  • This is a skill milestone, not just a mobility milestone.
Martial Arts — Intermediate

Rough belt equivalent

  • Karate: roughly green to blue belt.
  • Taekwondo: roughly green to blue belt.
  • Kung Fu: intermediate / green–blue equivalent where belts or sashes are used.

Belt colours vary substantially by organisation and school, so use your instructor’s progression standard where practical.

Requirement
  • Progress beyond the beginner milestone.
  • Demonstrate intermediate technique in your chosen martial art.
  • Use the progression standards of your actual martial-arts school where practical.
Sparring — Competent
Requirement
  • Demonstrate competent live sparring.
  • Maintain control and appropriate technique.
  • Spar at an appropriate intensity for your martial art and training environment.
